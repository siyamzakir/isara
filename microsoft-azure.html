Frequently Asked Questions

ISARA Advance™ on Microsoft Azure is a cryptographic posture management platform that helps organizations automatically discover cryptographic assets, identify weaknesses, quantify risk, and prioritize remediation across applications, infrastructure, and source code.

ISARA Advance scans applications, infrastructure, source code, and network environments to detect cryptographic algorithms, key lengths, configurations, and dependencies. It classifies these against current and post-quantum security standards to surface risks and prioritize remediation.

Within hours of deployment on Microsoft Azure, ISARA Advance begins delivering cryptographic visibility and risk insights. The platform is designed for rapid onboarding with minimal configuration required to start surfacing actionable findings.

ISARA Advance integrates with Azure Key Vault, Microsoft SQL Server, and Azure-connected certificate management workloads. Additional integration coverage expands continuously to support broader Azure service discovery.

CISOs, CIOs, boards, business unit leaders, application owners, GRC teams, security architects, engineers, and IT administrators all benefit. Each stakeholder gets role-appropriate visibility into cryptographic risk and remediation priorities.

ISARA Advance correlates cryptographic findings with business context, asset criticality, and risk scoring to surface the highest-priority remediation actions. Teams can filter, sort, and track remediation progress through dashboards aligned to executive and operational needs.
