A Confidential, High-Value Discussion Designed for CISOs

These dinners are built for leaders who need clarity on cryptographic risk, modernization strategy, and the realities of preparing large organizations for the post-quantum era. The format is tight, curated, and designed to spark meaningful peer-level insight.

What You Can Expect

  • Executive-Level Roundtable: 10-20 CISOs and senior leaders in a closed door, peer-driven format.
  • Research-Backed Discussion Topics: Cryptographic posture visibility, algorithm risk, hybrid certificates, PQC migration timelines, and governance challenges.
  • Cross-Industry Insight: Perspectives from government, financial services, energy, and critical infrastructure leaders dealing with long-life systems and compliance pressure.
  • Expert Facilitation: Hosted by ISARA's leadership and cryptography specialists to ensure accuracy, depth, and practicality.
  • Zero Sales Pitch: These sessions prioritize substance, not presentations or demos.

Who Attends

    This invite-only format is designed for executives responsible for securing cryptographic systems and long-term security strategy, including:
  • CISOs & Deputy CISOs
  • Directors of Information Security & Security Operations
  • Heads of Cryptography, PKI, or IAM
  • Security Architects & Engineering Leaders
  • Government & Critical Infrastructure Security Executives
  • Financial Services Security Leaders managing algorithm audits and regulatory pressure
