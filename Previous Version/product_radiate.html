Production-Ready Quantum Safe Cryptography

The ISARA Radiate™ Quantum-safe Toolkit is a high-performance, lightweight, and standards-based software development kit (SDK) designed for integrating quantum safety into your products today.

ISARA Radiate simplifies and accelerates your transition to quantum safety, offering clear benefits for engineers and business leaders alike.

Whether you're building a SaaS offering or a resource-constrained device, our toolkit provides the security and performance you need to protect yourself and your customers in the age of quantum computing.

Multiple Options for Complete Coverage

The Radiate Toolkit comes in two variants to meet the needs of diverse applications and environments.

Standard Radiate

Best for securing large-scale cloud applications, enterprise software, and general-purpose computing systems.

A full-featured, professional-grade SDK for developers building applications in traditional computing environments.

  • Production-Ready Quality: Our professional tools, extensive documentation, timely updates, and rigorous testing mean that you can deploy with confidence.
  • Copyleft-Free Code: Our optimized implementation of standardized PQC algorithms is free from copyleft licenses, protecting your intellectual property from accidental exposure to the public domain.
  • Enhanced Integrations: We have integrated our cryptographic implementations with OpenSSL and NSS, including the handling of standardized hybrid certificates. Integrate PQC into your products which use TLS.

An optimized, cutting-edge solution for securing devices with severe resource limitations, such as heapless ECUs and smartcards.

Embedded Radiate

Best for securing embedded systems and IoT devices where code size, memory footprint, and performance are critical.

  • Zero Heap Allocation: Requires no memory allocation on the heap, making it ideal for devices without operating systems or heap management. It can share scarce memory effectively without compromising performance.
  • Flexible Performance: Exceptional adaptable, offering speed-optimized, space-optimized, and balanced implementations to ensure optimal resource utilization for your specific use case.
  • Highest Standards: Our solution is MISRA-C Compliant and supports NIST PQC Security Level 1, 3, and 5 parameter sets, ensuring the highest levels of reliability and security.
